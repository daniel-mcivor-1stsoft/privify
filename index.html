Questions people ask before signing up

Something else? Email support@privify.co.uk.

How does text forwarding work?
Every text that arrives on your Privify number is emailed to the address on your account, showing who sent it and the full message. There's nothing to set up. It works as soon as your number is active.
How quickly will I get my number?
Your email aliases work the moment you sign up. Your UK number is usually assigned the moment you pay, and we text it to confirm it's live. Occasionally, when demand is high, it can take up to 24 hours, and we email you as soon as it's ready.
Will it work for bank codes and HMRC?
We provide genuine UK mobile numbers through an established UK telecoms provider, and most major services, including banks and HMRC, work well with them.
Can people call the number?
Yes. All calls go to voicemail and you can't answer live. You get the recording as an audio file in your email, and Premium writes it out as text too.
Can I send texts from the number?
On Premium, you can reply to anyone who has texted your number, up to 50 replies a month to UK mobiles. Replies come from your Privify number, so your real number stays hidden. You can't start new conversations, send bulk messages or make calls.
How do email aliases work?
You create an alias and give it out instead of your real address. Anything sent to it is forwarded to your inbox, labelled with the alias it came in on. You can reply from the alias, and switch it off any time so nothing more gets through.
Is the number permanently mine?
It's yours for as long as you're subscribed. We don't rotate or share numbers. If you cancel, the number is released.
Can I bring my existing number?
Yes, on Premium. Text PAC to 65075 to get your PAC code (networks must send it within 2 hours, free of charge), then port it to Privify. Ports usually complete within one working day. How to get a PAC code.
How does the free trial work?
Starter comes with 7 days free. Nothing is charged during the trial, then it's £1.99 a month. Cancel before the 7 days end and you pay nothing. Plus and Premium start billing straight away.
Is my data private and secure?
Messages and voicemail are stored on servers in the European Union. We comply with UK and EU GDPR, don't sell data and don't show adverts. Voicemail transcription runs on our own EU servers. See our Privacy Policy.
Do you have a plan for businesses?
Yes. Privify Business gives sole traders and small teams a UK work number with team logins, your own voicemail greeting, 200 text replies a month and VAT invoices, from £14.99 a month + VAT. See Privify for Business.
