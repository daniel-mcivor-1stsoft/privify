Questions

Things people ask
before signing up.

How does SMS forwarding work? +
Every text that arrives on your Privify number is forwarded to the email address on your account. The email shows who sent it and includes the full message. There is nothing to configure. It works as soon as your number is active.
Can I change the forwarding email? +
Yes. Update it any time from account settings. Changes take effect immediately.
How quickly will I get my number? +
Your email aliases work the moment you sign up. Your UK number is set up for you individually, so it works first time, and we email you as soon as it's live and ready to use.
Will it work for bank OTPs and HMRC? +
We provide genuine UK mobile numbers through an established UK telecoms provider, and most major services including banks and HMRC work well with them.
Can people call the number? +
Yes, people can call the number. All inbound calls go to voicemail and you cannot answer live. You get the recording as an audio file forwarded to your email. Automatic transcription to text is available on the Premium plan.
How do email aliases work? +
You request an alias and get a randomly generated address to give out instead of your real address. Any email sent to it is forwarded to your real inbox with a label showing which alias it arrived on. Delete the alias any time, and the sender gets a bounce and nothing more reaches you.
Can I reply from an alias? +
Yes. When an email is forwarded to your inbox from an alias, you can reply directly and it'll arrive to the sender from your alias address. Your real email stays hidden throughout.
Is the number permanently mine? +
Your number is yours for as long as you're subscribed. We don't rotate or share numbers. If you cancel, the number is released. Keep the subscription active and it stays yours.
Can I send texts from the number? +
Not at the moment. Privify is built for receiving.
Is my data private and secure? +
All messages and voicemails are stored on servers within the European Union. We comply with UK and EU GDPR, don't sell your data, and don't serve ads. Voicemail transcription runs entirely on our own EU infrastructure. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I port my existing number? +
Yes. Number porting is available on the Premium plan. If your number is still active, you can port it to Privify and keep receiving texts on it indefinitely. Text PAC to 65075 to get your PAC code. Networks are legally required to provide it within 2 hours, free of charge. Ports typically complete within one working day.
What is the identity monitor? +
We monitor known data breaches and credential leaks. If your email turns up in one, you get an alert straight away so you can change your passwords before anyone uses them.
How does the free trial work? +
The Starter plan comes with a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. After 7 days, billing starts at £1.99/month. Cancel any time before that and you pay nothing. Plus and Premium plans start billing immediately, no trial period.
Is there a browser extension? +
Yes. The Privify extension for Chrome detects email and phone fields as you browse and lets you drop in an alias or your Privify number with one click, with no copying and pasting from the dashboard. It's free with every plan and only activates on forms you choose to fill.
