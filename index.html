Email aliases

The same idea,
for your inbox.

The same reason you have a private number — you hand it to services without revealing the real thing. Email aliases do exactly that for your inbox. Use a fresh one for each service, delete it whenever you like.

How it works

Request an alias and you get a randomly generated address in seconds. Any email sent to it lands in your real inbox with a clear label showing which alias it came in on. Delete the alias any time and the emails stop — permanently.

Your real address stays hidden

The service never sees your real email address. If their database is breached or they sell your data, the worst that happens is you delete the alias. No damage to your real inbox, no need to update passwords anywhere else.

See who's selling your data

Use one alias per service and you always know the source. When spam arrives, the alias it came in on tells you exactly who leaked or sold your data — even if they deny it. One click to delete the alias and cut them off for good.

Kill spam at the source

Unlike unsubscribe links — which often confirm your address is live — deleting an alias is permanent and silent. Nothing gets confirmed. Nothing gets added to another list. The sender gets a bounce and that's it.

UK Digital Identity

The UK is building a
digital identity system.
Are you in control?

The government's Digital Identity and Attributes Trust Framework (DIATF) is already live. Banks, landlords, employers, and public services are moving toward verified digital identity — using your phone number and email address as the anchor points.

That makes your real contact details more valuable to protect than ever. Once your number or email is over-shared or compromised, it's connected to your identity permanently.

Privify doesn't opt you out of digital identity — it gives you a controlled layer between your real details and the services that ask for them.

Questions

Things people ask
before signing up.

How does SMS forwarding work? +
Every text that arrives on your Privify number is forwarded to the email address on your account. The email shows who sent it and includes the full message. There is nothing to configure — it works as soon as your number is active.
Can I change the forwarding email? +
Yes — update it any time from account settings. Changes take effect immediately.
What's the setup fee for? +
The £12 one-time setup fee covers provisioning your dedicated UK mobile number. Charged once when your number is activated. Your monthly subscription covers everything else.
How quickly will I get my number? +
Numbers are typically assigned within 24 hours of signing up. We set each one up individually to make sure everything works correctly.
Will it work for bank OTPs and HMRC? +
We provide genuine UK mobile numbers through an established UK telecoms provider, and most major services including banks and HMRC work well with them. You have a 14-day statutory right to cancel — if it doesn't work for your use case, contact us at support@privify.co.uk.
Can people call the number? +
Yes, people can call the number. All inbound calls go to voicemail and you cannot answer live. You get the recording as an audio file forwarded to your email. Automatic transcription to text is available on the Premium plan.
Is the number permanently mine? +
Your number is yours for as long as you're subscribed. We don't rotate or share numbers. If you cancel, your number is released — if you want to keep it long-term, keep the subscription active.
Can I send texts from the number? +
Not at the moment. Privify is built for receiving. Outbound SMS is something we are working on. If it matters to you, mention it when you sign up.
Is my data private and secure? +
All messages and voicemails are stored on servers within the European Union. We comply with UK and EU GDPR, don't sell your data, and don't serve ads. Voicemail transcription runs entirely on our own EU infrastructure. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I port my existing number? +
Yes — if your number is still active, you can port it to Privify and keep receiving texts on it indefinitely. Text PAC to 65075 to get your PAC code. Networks are legally required to provide it within 2 hours, free of charge. Ports typically complete within one working day. Standard £12 setup fee applies.
How do email aliases work? +
You request an alias and get a randomly generated address to give out instead of your real address. Any email sent to it is forwarded to your real inbox with a label showing which alias it arrived on. Delete the alias any time — the sender gets a bounce and nothing more reaches you.
Can I reply from an alias? +
Not currently. Aliases are for receiving only — the same as the phone numbers. Outbound reply support is on the roadmap. If this matters to you, let us know when you sign up.
What is the identity monitor? +
We monitor known data breaches and credential leaks. If your email address appears in a breach, you get an immediate alert so you can change your passwords before attackers exploit them.
How does the free trial work? +
The Starter plan comes with a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged a penny during the trial. After 7 days, billing starts at £1.99/month. You can cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be billed. Plus and Premium plans start immediately with no trial period.
