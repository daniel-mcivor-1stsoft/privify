Questions

Things businesses ask
before signing up.

Can sole traders use Business? +
Yes. Business is for anyone using their number for work, whether you work on your own or with a team. You don't need a limited company.
Do I need a company number? +
Only if you're a limited company or LLP. We check it with Companies House when you sign up. Sole traders and partnerships just give a trading name and address.
Do you provide VAT invoices? +
Yes. Every invoice shows our VAT number, and yours if you add it at checkout. Business prices are shown without VAT, which is added at 20%.
Can my team have their own logins? +
Yes. Invite people by email and they sign in with their own account. Give them their own number or access to the business number, and choose who sees what.
What happens when someone leaves? +
Remove them in your dashboard. Their access ends straight away, and anything sent to the business number keeps reaching the rest of the team.
Can we keep our existing number? +
Yes. Porting is included, so you can move an existing UK mobile number to Privify with a PAC code. Ports usually complete within one working day.
Can we make calls from the number? +
No. Callers hear your greeting and leave a voicemail, which arrives as an audio file with a transcript. You can reply to texts, but you can't make calls.
Can we send marketing texts? +
No. Replies are for answering people who have texted you. Marketing, bulk and unsolicited messages aren't allowed, and sending limits apply. See our Acceptable Use Policy.
Is there a free trial? +
No. Business starts billing when you sign up. Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum, then roll monthly and you can cancel any time.
Can we pay yearly? +
Yes. Pay yearly and you get two months free: £149.90 + VAT a year instead of £179.88. Team members on a yearly plan are £79.90 or £29.90 a year. Yearly plans are paid up front and run to the end of the year. Extra text replies are still billed monthly.
Where is our data stored? +
All messages and voicemails are stored on servers within the European Union. We comply with UK and EU GDPR, don't sell data and don't serve ads. See our Privacy Policy.
