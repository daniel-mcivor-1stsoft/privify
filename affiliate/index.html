FAQ

Questions

Is there a minimum audience size?

No. We work with creators at all sizes. A highly engaged newsletter with 500 subscribers in the right niche is more valuable than a broad account with 100k followers.

How is attribution tracked?

Your unique link adds a ref parameter to the checkout URL. We store this in the visitor's browser for 30 days — so even if they don't sign up immediately, you get credit if they convert within that window.

When and how do you pay?

We review and pay out monthly. Payment is by bank transfer (UK accounts) or PayPal. There's no minimum threshold — even a single conversion gets paid.

Do I need to be a Privify customer?

You don't have to be, but we do prefer affiliates who've actually tried the product — it makes for more genuine content and better conversion. We'll give you a free account when you're approved.

Are there content restrictions?

We ask that all content is honest and accurate. You can't make claims about Privify that aren't true, and all sponsored content must be clearly disclosed under UK ASA guidelines.
