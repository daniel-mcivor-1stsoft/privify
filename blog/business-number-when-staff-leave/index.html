What happens to your business number when someone leaves?

November 2026 · 5 min read

In a lot of small businesses, staff use their own mobiles for work. Customers save that number, text it to rebook, and pass it on to friends. Supplier accounts send login codes to it.

When that person leaves, the number goes with them, and so do the conversations, the customer relationships and sometimes the only way into an account. Here's how to avoid that.

Whose number is it?

If a number is on someone's own contract, it's theirs. They can keep it, switch it to a new provider, or take it to their next job, and the customers who text it go with them. A number stays with your business only if the business controls it.

What leaves with a personal number

  • Customers. They keep texting the number they know, which now belongs to someone who no longer works for you.
  • Message history. Quotes, bookings and agreements sit on a phone you don't have.
  • Logins. Accounts set up with that mobile keep sending codes to it.
  • Adverts and listings. Anywhere the number appears now points to the wrong person.

Give staff business-owned numbers from day one

The fix is simple: anything customers or accounts use should be a number the business controls. That can be one shared business number the team reads together, or a separate number for each person that still belongs to the business.

When someone leaves, you remove their access. The number, its history and its logins stay with you.

A leaver checklist

  • Remove their access to every business number and shared inbox.
  • List the accounts that send codes to their personal mobile, and move each one to a business number.
  • Check adverts, listings, email signatures and invoices for their personal number.
  • Decide whether their work number keeps receiving messages and who reads them, or is closed.

Do it before anyone leaves. Moving accounts and customers to business numbers is much easier while everyone is still around to approve changes and receive codes.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns a business mobile number if an employee leaves?

If the number is on the employee's own contract, it's theirs and they can take it with them. To keep a number with the business, it needs to be on a contract or account the business controls.

Can I keep a former employee's work number?

Only if the business owns it. If it's on their personal contract you'll usually need to agree a transfer with them, which is why it's best to give staff business-owned numbers from the start.

What should I change when someone leaves?

Remove their access to business numbers and inboxes, move any accounts that send codes to their personal mobile onto a business number, and update any adverts or listings that show their personal number.
