WhatsApp now supports two accounts on one phone. Here's how to get the second number

November 2026 · 5 min read

WhatsApp now lets you run two accounts in the same app on one phone. Android users have had it since 2023, and in 2026 it finally reached the iPhone. You can switch between a personal account and a work or side-hustle account without a second phone or a separate app.

There's one catch: every WhatsApp account needs its own phone number. Here's how the feature works, and how to get that second number without buying a second SIM.

What the multi-account feature does

  • Two accounts, one app. Each account has its own chats, groups, profile photo and status.
  • Quick switching. You swap between them from the settings menu, rather than logging out and back in.
  • Labelled notifications. Notifications show which account a message arrived on, so you know whether it's a friend or a customer before you open it.
  • Separate privacy settings. Each account has its own settings for profile photo, last seen and who can add you to groups.

How to add a second account

  1. Update WhatsApp to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open Settings. On iPhone, look under Account; on Android, tap the arrow next to your name.
  3. Choose Add account.
  4. Enter the second phone number and type in the verification code WhatsApp sends to it.
  5. Set up the profile for the new account. You'll then see a switcher to move between the two.

The rollout has been gradual, especially on iPhone. If you don't see Add account yet, update the app and check again in a few days.

The catch: you need a second number

WhatsApp identifies every account by its phone number and verifies it by sending a code. So the second account needs a number that is:

  • Yours, not one shared with other people online.
  • Able to receive texts, so the verification code arrives.
  • Kept long term. WhatsApp can ask you to verify again later, for example when you get a new phone. If you no longer have the number, you lose the account.

The usual answer is a second SIM or eSIM. That means another contract or pay-as-you-go top-ups, a phone that supports dual SIM, and another number to keep active. There's a simpler option.

Don't use free "receive SMS online" sites. The numbers on those sites are public, so anyone can read the codes. Someone else could take over your WhatsApp account by asking for a new code to the same number. We explain why in receiving SMS online in the UK.
